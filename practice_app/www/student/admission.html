Admission Process Timeline

01
01 → Explore Program

02 → Check Eligibility

Verify minimum academic aggregate scores (60%+ in Higher Secondary / 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) or valid GATE scores for postgraduates.

03
03 → Apply Online

Fill out the official EDU Portal online application form, upload academic marksheets, and submit the processing fee online.

Required Documents Checklist
  • 10th & 12th Standard Mark Sheets (Original + 3 Copies)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
  • Community Certificate / Category Reservation Certificate (if applicable)
  • National Entrance Exam Scorecard (JEE Main / GATE / State Rank Card)
  • Passport-size Recent Color Photographs (6 Copies)
  • Aadhaar Card / Identity Verification Proof
